Scope: the marketing landing page at `/`. Visitor mode: **Persuade**. The app
itself (library + reader) moves to `/library` and keeps Operate mode; this brief
does not govern it.

Audience and job: someone arriving cold, usually from a shared link, holding a
"what if" premise. They must understand in one viewport that this turns one
changed rule into an illustrated twelve-page chapter, and leave by either
opening the atlas or reading the fixture world for free.

Proof and content: the only real evidence is `fixtures/chapter-1.ts` — "The Long
Fall", rule *gravity is half as strong*. Its page text, chain, styleBible,
palette, arc and through-line are quoted verbatim on the page. The measured cost
figure (172.80 neurons per plate, ~4.8 chapters/day) is real. There are no users,
testimonials, press or benchmarks and none may be invented. No plate images are
checked in, so the landing's artwork is authored flat-vector scenes drawn to the
fixture's declared style bible, labelled on-page as illustrative rather than
model output.

Constraints: the visual world is settled and inherited, not replaced — Fraunces
/ Newsreader / IBM Plex Mono, the dim instrument chrome from `app/globals.css`,
the artwork as the only warm thing on screen. Copy keeps the app's quiet,
unhyped register. `prefers-reduced-motion` and a phone viewport both fall back to
a plain vertical document with no 3D.

Chosen direction: **The Turning Book** (surface round `befd8407`, dealt index 5,
locked by the user over Recto/Verso and The Plate Corridor). Scrolling turns
leaves — each lifts, rotates about a left-hand spine and lays away, revealing the
next. The product's own reading gesture is the site's only navigation.

Memorable moment: the leaf turn itself — a raking highlight sweeping across the
sheet as it rotates, its vellum back showing through, the read pile thickening at
the spine while the unread edge-stack on the right thins from eleven to none.

Unresolved: whether the fixture auto-load link (`/library?read=long-fall`) should
also exist as a shareable permalink to a specific plate.
